# Requirements

## Functional Requirements
- **Citizen Portal**: Allow citizens to submit, view, and track community issues.
- **Authority Dashboard**: Provide an interface for local authorities to manage, update, and resolve reported issues.
- **Issue Tracking Workflow**: Track issues through states (e.g., Submitted, In Progress, Resolved).
- **Transparency**: Public visibility into common issues and resolution metrics (optional/MVP+).

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **Usability**: Accessible, responsive, and intuitive modern web design.
- **Security**: Role-based access control for citizens vs. authorities.
- **Reliability**: High uptime for public service availability.
